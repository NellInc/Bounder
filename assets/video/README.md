# Bounder explainer

Published 3 October 2026. A two-minute, 1920 × 1080 film, with English captions
and a transcript on the homepage.

Copyright 2026 Nell Watson, Inc. All rights reserved. The film and its poster
are excluded from the repository’s Apache-2.0 licence.

The original instrumental was generated and officially downloaded under a
Suno Pro subscription. Narration, illustrative shots and keyframes use
Gemini, Veo, Seedance and Sunburst. Manim, Motion Canvas, Hyperframes and
FFmpeg were used for the diagrams, composition and assembly.

The film describes the simulation-only Bounder testbed.
