## Summary

Bounder is a drone geofencing technology company. The website (bounder.io) serves as the primary marketing presence, providing product information, contact, legal pages, and SEO-optimized public content. (CLAUDE.md)

## Product Domain

Bounder.io addresses drone geofencing — technology that restricts where drones can fly by defining virtual geographic boundaries. The site markets this capability to potential customers and partners.
