Protection rules

Constraints, not targeting logic.

Bounder does not decide whom or what to engage. It enforces narrow, reviewed constraints on whether a requested physical state change may proceed.

◎

Human protection

Civilian presence, medical facilities, schools, cultural sites, declared protected zones, surrender, and other protected status can require denial or escalation.

◇

Friendly-force protection

Authenticated friendly positions and minimum-separation rules can prevent blue-on-blue incidents without exposing arbitrary control authority.

⌁

Prosaic operating rules

Time windows, altitude, weather, battery, communications, identification, operator authentication, geography, and policy freshness remain equally important.

Conceptual safety demonstrator.

These examples are deliberately abstract. Operational rules require lawful authority, expert review, reliable sensing, rigorous verification, and human accountability. Bounder is not certified safety software or an engagement system.
