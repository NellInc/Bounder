Why Bounder

A narrow component with a serious job.

Bounder does not plan missions or choose goals. It sits between decision software and physical actuation, checking whether a requested change is authorized, current, and within policy.

People

Protect people

Civilian, medical, school, cultural, surrender, and declared protected status can stop an otherwise valid command.

Separation

Prevent friendly harm

Authenticated friendly positions and minimum separation rules reduce blue-on-blue risk at the final point of control.

Operations

Enforce ordinary limits

Altitude, time, weather, communications, battery, identity, geography, and policy freshness are first-class constraints.

Demonstrator, not deployment assurance.

The displayed decisions come from deterministic Go interlock receipts and a signed Fleet contract published with their JSON schemas. Bounder is a simulation-only reference, not certified safety software or an engagement system.
